## Featured research and engineering

### SERPS — Secure Examination, Smart Test Player & Intelligent Proctor

**Flagship research-oriented, production-oriented prototype**

[Explore SERPS](https://github.com/basil-emeokoro/SERPS) · [Architecture and engineering](https://github.com/basil-emeokoro/SERPS/blob/main/docs/portfolio/engineering-case-study.md) · [Evaluation and reproducibility](https://github.com/basil-emeokoro/SERPS/blob/main/docs/portfolio/evaluation-and-reproducibility.md) · [Doctoral research directions](https://github.com/basil-emeokoro/SERPS/blob/main/docs/portfolio/phd-research-directions.md)

SERPS investigates an explainable identity-assurance, multimodal monitoring, and institutional-governance layer for digital assessment. Its research architecture links bounded local observations to structured evidence, contextual reasoning, policy evaluation, and accountable human review.

**Engineering focus:** Next.js/React, FastAPI, structured evidence workflows, contextual decision support, role-aware governance, and auditable review.

**Evidence boundary:** SERPS is a **research prototype**, not a certified secure browser, a complete CBT platform, a production-validated biometric system, or an autonomous misconduct adjudicator. Historical tests and unresolved acceptance requirements are documented in the project repository.

## Research agenda

**1. Trustworthy multimodal AI for human assessment.** Explore how visual, audio, behavioral, and contextual signals can be interpreted without reducing uncertain observations to unsupported misconduct conclusions. Research questions include explanation quality, calibration, fairness, privacy, and human–AI decision reliability.

**2. Handwriting-centered human–AI assessment.** Investigate digital ink, handwriting recognition, multimodal response capture, formative feedback, and learning analytics, with particular attention to psychometric validity and accessibility.

**3. Measurement-informed AI evaluation.** Connect classical test theory, item response theory, error analysis, uncertainty, and subgroup evaluation to the validation of educational AI systems.
