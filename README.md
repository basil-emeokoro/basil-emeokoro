## About Me

I am an interdisciplinary researcher and engineer working at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, **Explainable AI**, **Educational Technology**, **Educational Assessment**, **Psychometrics**, **Data Science**, **Responsible AI**, **Healthcare AI**, and **Research Methods**.

I currently serve as an **Educational Assessment Administrator** at **The West African Examinations Council (WAEC), Nigeria**. I previously worked in **Psychometrics and Test Development**, and I now support educational assessment administration while continuing research in AI-enabled assessment systems, digital assessment, and responsible decision-support technologies.

My work combines research design, statistical thinking, machine learning engineering, privacy-aware data workflows, explainability, and reproducible software systems.

---

## Research Vision

I am interested in designing trustworthy, explainable, and responsible AI systems that improve decision-making in education, healthcare, and the public sector. My long-term goal is to contribute to research at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Educational Technology**, **Psychometrics**, **Digital Assessment**, and **Responsible AI**, with particular emphasis on **fairness**, **transparency**, **privacy**, and **evidence-based decision-making**.

I am especially motivated by problems where AI must be technically strong, socially responsible, interpretable to stakeholders, and grounded in rigorous research evidence.
